# Altered Nadeo maps

**Ten maps in this repository are official Nadeo campaign maps that someone
modified and republished — so each one has a second leaderboard, on the same
geometry, with tens of thousands of players on it instead of a few dozen.**

An Altered Nadeo map takes an official campaign map and changes one thing: the
route is reversed, the finish is moved onto an early checkpoint, or a surface is
swapped. The layout underneath is the official one, which means the official
map's field has already driven your corners — usually far better, and always in
much larger numbers.

## What changes about driving, by category

**Geometry and surface both preserved.** Same blocks, same road, same physics.
The official field's lines *and* times carry straight over: an official replay is
a usable reference for how the corner is taken and how long it should take.

**CP1 End.** The altered map is the official map's opening with the finish moved
onto its first checkpoint. This is the best case — hundreds of thousands of
players have driven exactly your race, at full commitment, as the start of their
own lap, and their CP1 split is directly your finishing time.

**Reverse.** Same road and same physics, but the official field drove it the
other way. Their replays tell you where the road is and how wide the usable
corridor is. They do not tell you the line, and their times mean nothing here.

**Surface swap** (210218 only: wet icy wood in place of tech road). The structure
is the official map's, the road surface is not. Grip, cornering speed, braking
distance and therefore the whole line all change. Use the official field for
geometry only — never a time, never a sector minimum, never a pointwise envelope.

## Two results worth studying

**270051 — the run here beats the official field.** The official top five on
Fall 2025 - 16 reach CP1 in 4.932, 4.951, 4.951, 4.962 and 4.966. The tape in
this repository does the same stretch in **4.830** — faster than any of them, on
a field of 87 596 players rather than the 903 on the altered board.

**228607 — the launcher nobody on the altered board found.** All fifteen of the
official Fall 2024 - 08 top fifteen fire the launcher, at 692–997 km/h. **None of
the 23 players on the altered leaderboard ever did.** The technique was never
undiscovered; it was sitting on a leaderboard nobody had connected to the map.

That is the general point of this folder. On these ten maps the small
leaderboard is not the state of the art — the official one is, and it is one to
four orders of magnitude larger.
