# The first full re-simulation sweep — 2026-08-24

*Every banked result in the repo, back through the plain oracle, in 3 minutes.
This is the check the project's rules have always required and nothing had ever
performed.*

```
174 tapes · 36 maps · 94 map files registered
112 hold · 0 differ · 1 DNF · 1 STARTED-ELSEWHERE · 13 refused (human) · 47 UNMEASURED
```

**Nothing published is wrong about its own time.** Every one of the 112 tapes
the sweep could measure re-simulated at exactly the millisecond it is published
under. That is the headline and it is a real result: `0 differ`.

The other four numbers are each worth a paragraph.

---

## 1 DNF — and the filename already said so

`spaghetti nights 2 / SEGMENT_cp5_32702_DO_NOT_PUBLISH_declares_40226.Ghost.Gbx`
→ **DNF at cp5, published as 40.226**.

A segment map's tape, named `DO_NOT_PUBLISH`, declaring a time it does not
achieve. The sweep is agreeing with the filename, which is the correct answer
and also a small control: the detector found the one file in the corpus that
was already known to be this shape, and did not find any others.

## 1 STARTED-ELSEWHERE — a cut tape, behaving as a cut tape

`magnet trial / CUT_795034.Ghost.Gbx` → its telemetry starts **1089.2 m** from
the start line, simulating 795.034.

This is the retries-spliced-out cut of a long trial run. A cut tape's telemetry
legitimately does not begin at the start line, so this is the check working
rather than a defect found — **but it is exactly the same reading a genuinely
mis-started run produces**, and the sweep cannot tell them apart. Two tapes,
one benign and one not, are indistinguishable to this instrument. That is
stated here rather than suppressed with an exception for `CUT_*`, because an
exception is how the next real one gets missed.

## 13 refused — the no-ghost gate, doing its job

Thirteen human recordings in the repo were refused by the gate rather than
read. They are published as context for earlier work and they are not
evaluation references.

## 47 UNMEASURED — and this is the finding worth acting on

**Forty-seven of 174 banked tapes, better than a quarter of the corpus, cannot
be placed by provenance and are therefore not covered by the standing
re-simulation.** Every one has the same reason: nothing in the filename says
whether we made it or a human drove it — `best_39961.Ghost.Gbx`,
`WIP_keyboard.Ghost.Gbx`, `AK_15277.Ghost.Gbx`, `SPLICE_23498.Ghost.Gbx`.

The gate fails **closed**: an unplaceable file is refused, never assumed to be
ours. That is the right default and it is not the fix.

**The fix is not to widen the list of recognised prefixes.** Guessing that
`best_*` is probably ours is precisely the reasoning that leaks a human
recording into an evaluation, and the no-ghost rule is the one rule in this
project that cannot be traded against convenience. The fix is the `PROV` record
in `DESIGN.md` §2.2 — provenance carried *by the tape*, not inferred from its
name — after which this number should go to zero and stay there. Until then the
honest statement is:

> The standing re-simulation covers 112 of 174 banked tapes. The other 62 are
> refused or unplaceable, and the sweep says which and why on every pass.

## What this run also established about the instrument

* **Every tape gets a row, including the ones never measured.** The first
  version of this sweep wrote results only for tapes that reached the oracle —
  114 rows for 174 tapes — so a log that had never looked at a quarter of the
  corpus read as a clean sweep. An absent row is not a passing row.
* **The start-position check is live**, two-sided: at a tolerance of 0.001 m a
  real TAS tape fires and reports 10.6 m — a car's offset from the centre of
  its start block, which is the magnitude you would predict — and at 32 m it is
  silent.
* **94 of 94 registered map files verify by md5**, and one appended byte reads
  as `CHANGED`.
